About

Big industries, real stakes.

My work spans healthcare payments, e-commerce, and fintech, large industries where the small details matter to people: a medical bill someone can finally understand, a checkout that works, access to wages someone has spent their days earning.

Why I made the jump.

I spent years as a designer, and a few months ago I shifted into a PM role to get closer to how AI products actually get built. I wanted to be in the technical decisions, not just the screens. The design background doesn't go anywhere, it's the lens I bring to strategy, flows, and the details many roadmaps skip over.

Outside of work.

You'll find me running, on a tennis court, or chasing whatever new thing has my attention that month. I have a lot of interests and not enough weekends. I also enjoy sports, reading, and trying to keep up with my favorite podcasts and newsletters.
