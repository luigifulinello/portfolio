Case Study Sections

The available content sections in narrative order — Overview → Approach → Showcase → The Work → Reflection → Impact — plus optional modules. Two Impact variants: quantitative (figures) and a qualitative version for when numbers aren't shareable. For image, gallery, and hero modules see Image Layouts.

Impact — Qualitative New

Classes: .cs-outcomes · .cs-outcome

For when figures aren't available or shareable (NDA, pre-launch, confidential). Same three-up rhythm as the metrics block, but bold outcome statements with an optional directional marker (↑ ↓ →) instead of numbers. The marker uses the case study's brand accent.

Impact

↑

Faster path to purchase

Shoppers reached the buy box with fewer detours — confirmed in session replays and moderated tests.

→

Adopted as the standard

The pattern rolled out across the brand portfolio and became the default for new product pages.

↓

Fewer support tickets

Clearer information and expectations cut the "where do I…" questions reaching the support team.

Takeaways Optional

Classes: .cs-takeaways · .cs-takeaway

A scannable 3-up alternative to the Reflection prose — three crisp lessons.

Ship the cheapest win first

Early, low-cost wins earn the trust to fund the harder work.

Speak in the data leaders own

Framing recommendations in revenue terms gets them approved.

Constraints sharpen scope

Fewer resources force a cleaner, more ruthless set of changes.
