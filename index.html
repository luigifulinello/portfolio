About

Big industries, real stakes.

E-commerce, healthcare payments, fintech: industries where the details are the business. A checkout that doesn't make you hesitate. A medical bill you can finally understand. What I've learned: craft and conversion aren't a tradeoff. At a multi-brand DTC portfolio I owned both design and the A/B program, so every design decision answered to a metric.

A PM who designs

I run product like a PM and build like a designer: I set the roadmap, define the success metrics, then prototype the thing itself, starting in an LLM, building in code, finishing in Figma. No translation loss between strategy and screen. It means the details that decide conversion, the empty states, the error messages, the sentence that keeps a customer moving, are owned by the same person accountable for the number.

Outside of work.

You'll find me running, on a tennis court, and getting outdoors with my family. I also enjoy sports, reading, and trying to keep up with my favorite podcasts and newsletters.
