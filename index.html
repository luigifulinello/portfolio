About

Big industries, real stakes.

E-commerce, healthcare payments, fintech: industries where the details are the business. A checkout that doesn't make you hesitate. A medical bill you can finally understand. What I've learned is craft and conversion aren't a tradeoff.

A PM who designs

I run product like a PM and build like a designer. I set the roadmap, define the metrics, then prototype the experience in code with AI tools. No translation loss between strategy and screen.

Strong opinions, tested.

I owned both design and the A/B program at a multi-brand DTC portfolio, so I learned to treat every strong opinion as a hypothesis. I form the bet, ship the test, and let conversion, revenue, and retention have the last word.
